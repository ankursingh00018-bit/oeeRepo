# Document 8 — Maintenance Inspection Checklist

Purpose: Used by the technician performing physical inspection after
the Agent's recommendation is approved. Generates the maintenance
instructions attached to a Work Order.

## Before Inspection (All Machines)

- [ ] Controlled stop performed (no abrupt e-stop unless already
      triggered).
- [ ] Lockout/tagout (LOTO) applied by a qualified technician.
- [ ] Confirm zero-energy state (no residual hydraulic pressure,
      spindle fully stopped, robot in safe-stop).
- [ ] Review the Agent's incident report and probable cause before
      starting, so inspection is targeted.

## Rotating Equipment Checklist (M-101, M-102, M-103)

- [ ] Check for bearing noise (growling, grinding) by manual rotation
      if safe to do so.
- [ ] Check for radial/axial bearing play by hand.
- [ ] Inspect lubrication: level, color, consistency, contamination.
- [ ] Inspect bearing housing for heat discoloration.
- [ ] Check shaft/joint alignment.
- [ ] Check mounting bolts and housing integrity.
- [ ] (M-103 only) Inspect wire feed rollers and tension mechanism
      separately from joint servo — do not conflate the two systems.

## Process Equipment Checklist — Injection Molding (M-104)

- [ ] Check heater band resistance on flagged zone(s).
- [ ] Verify thermocouple accuracy against a reference probe.
- [ ] Inspect mold for wear, flash lines, vent blockage.
- [ ] Check clamp force calibration.
- [ ] Confirm resin dryness/moisture content.

## Process Equipment Checklist — Hydraulic Press (M-105)

- [ ] Inspect die mounting bolts and alignment against reference
      marks.
- [ ] Check hydraulic oil condition and temperature.
- [ ] Inspect hydraulic seals for leakage.
- [ ] Verify light curtain and two-hand control function before
      returning to service.

## After Maintenance — Verification Readings (All Machines)

- [ ] Vibration returned to Normal band (machine-specific, see
      Machine Manual).
- [ ] Temperature returned to Normal band.
- [ ] Reject rate returned below Normal threshold.
- [ ] Production rate returned to or above baseline.
- [ ] Sustain readings across at least one full production cycle
      before sign-off.
- [ ] Record final root cause and corrective action taken for the
      Incident Report and Maintenance History log.

## Sign-off

- [ ] Technician name and ID recorded.
- [ ] Time of completion recorded.
- [ ] Machine returned to Supervisor for release to production.
